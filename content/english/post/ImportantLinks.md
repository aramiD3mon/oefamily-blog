Medicaid and Payer Sources

- [AHCCCS](https://www.azahcccs.gov/)

Hospitals and medical care

- [Mercy Care provider directory](https://www.mercycareaz.org/providers/index.html)

Emergency and public safety
- Maricopa County Public Health
- City of Phoenix emergency and public safety pages
- Arizona Department of Emergency and Military Affairs

Utilities and facility operations
- APS, SRP, and local utility providers — electric service, outage maps, billing, and start/stop service.
- City water utilities for the exact facility location — water, sewer, and trash service.
- Gas provider site — if the property uses natural gas for heating or cooking.

Pricing and market research
- CareScout cost-of-care report — useful for benchmarking assisted living pricing by state and market.
- A Place for Mom cost guide — broad market pricing reference for assisted living costs.
- Senior care cost guides for Arizona and Phoenix — helpful for competitive pricing research, though they should be treated as secondary sources.

Staffing and workforce
- Arizona Department of Economic Security — hiring support, unemployment, wage guidance, and workforce tools.
- Arizona State Board of Nursing / care-related licensing boards — verify nurse credentialing and scope issues.
- Maricopa workforce and training resources — for recruiting caregivers, med techs, and administrative staff.

 Facility operations and resident support
- Electronic health record / resident management platform — for assessments, care plans, meds, incident logs, and family communication.
- Background check and fingerprinting portals — for hiring compliance.
- Ombudsman and elder advocacy resources — for resident rights, complaints, and quality oversight.
