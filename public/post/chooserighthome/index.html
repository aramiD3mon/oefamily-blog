How to Choose the Right Assisted Living Home

  • 28/3/2026
  • 2-minute read

Choosing an assisted living home for a loved one is one of the most important decisions a family can make. In Maricopa County, Arizona, there are dozens of options — from large corporate facilities to intimate residential care homes like Oak & Ember. Knowing what to look for can make all the difference.

Start with the right questions before you even schedule a tour, it helps to clarify your loved one’s specific needs:

  • are they primarily independent but need help with medications and meals?
  • do they require memory care support? Understanding the level of care needed will narrow your search significantly
  • what is the staff-to-resident ratio during the day and overnight?
  • is there a Registered Nurse on staff or on call 24/7?
  • how are medications managed and administered?
  • what activities and social programming are offered?
  • what is the process for handling medical emergencies?

The Tour - What to Look For: A tour tells you far more than a brochure ever could. Pay attention to how staff interact with current residents-are they warm, patient and attentive? Does the home smell clean and feel welcoming? Are residents engaged and comfortable or do they seem is “The best care homes feel like a home first and a facility second. Licensing and Compliance in Arizona: All assisted living homes in Arizona must be licensed by the Arizona Department of Health Services (ADHS). You can look up any facility’s inspection history and compliance record on the ADHS website. This is a critical step that many families ov Ask to see the facility’s most recent inspection report. A reputable home will share this willingly. Look for patterns in any cited deficiencies-occasional minor issues are normal, but repeated violations around medication management or resident safety are red flags.

Small vs. Large Facilities - Why Size Matters: Larger facilities offer more amenities, but smaller residential care homes often provide a higher quality of individualized care.
