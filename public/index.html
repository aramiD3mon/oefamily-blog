ARIZONA'S COST OF ASSISTED LIVING 2026

Cost of Assisted Living in Arizona Arizona assisted living costs are about $6,370 per month in 2026 or roughly $73,000–$79,800 per year, which is above the national median. Pricing varies by city, care level and service model, with Phoenix-area communities often costing more and some southern or western Arizona locations costing less.
